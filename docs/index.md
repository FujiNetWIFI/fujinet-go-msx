# FujiNet Go MSX — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-08-18_

FujiNet Go MSX is a MSX emulator with built-in FujiNet network
device support, published by the FujiNet project.

## Data collection

**FujiNet Go MSX collects no personal data.** The app contains no analytics,
no advertising, no tracking identifiers, and transmits nothing to the
developer or any third party on its own.

## Network access

The app uses the network solely for:

- **User-directed FujiNet connections** — TNFS servers, network mounts, and
  other hosts that *you* configure inside the emulated machine or the FujiNet
  configuration interface. The app never connects anywhere you did not ask
  it to.
- **The local loopback web interface** (127.0.0.1) used to configure the
  built-in FujiNet device on your own phone or tablet.

## Files you import

System ROMs, cartridges, and disk or tape images that you import are copied
into the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded,
shared, or transmitted anywhere.

## Accounts and identity

There are no user accounts. The app never asks for, stores, or transmits any
identity information.

## Data retention

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.
